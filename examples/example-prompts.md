# Example prompts

Attach a readable paper and use one of these prompts.

## Full reading

> Read this psychology paper. First identify its type and readable components, then output **Full Analysis**. Use Chinese by default, retain English technical terms, and attach source locators to important claims.

## Content module only

> Output **Chunk 1 — Content Analysis** for this paper. Explain the introduction's logical chain, the purpose of the analysis method, results by hypothesis, and how the discussion responds to them. Do not infer unavailable data.

## Academic English study

> Output **Chunk 2 — Academic Language** only. Select useful academic-English patterns from the introduction and discussion, explain them in Chinese, and give reusable templates. Include a bilingual glossary for the psychological and neuroscience terms that appear.

## Citation tracing

> Output **Chunk 3 — Citation Traceability** only. Trace the paper's support for the claim: "[insert claim]". Show the relevant source passage location, in-text citations, and matching reference-list entries. Do not claim the cited papers independently prove it unless their full text is supplied.

## Critical reading without external search

> Output **Chunk 4 — Critical Reading** only. Map results to discussion claims, identify paper-visible limitations, and propose paper-grounded next steps. Do not search externally.

## Further innovation with explicit authorization

> I authorize external literature searching. My topic is "[topic]". Output **Chunk 4 — Critical Reading**, including evidence-backed further innovation directions. Clearly distinguish the supplied paper from external sources.

## First interaction menu

When a user has uploaded a paper but has not selected a scope, first provide the identification card and then offer the five formal option names with concise function descriptions in the user's primary language. For example, an English-language user should receive English descriptions; a Chinese-language user should receive Chinese descriptions. Do not translate the formal option names unless the target agent requires localized routing labels.
